The Code

Sharp writing needs a strong spine. Enchanted Daze distinguishes reporting, opinion, satire, and art while respecting the people and communities whose stories make this place.

Report what can be proven.

Facts are sourced, claims are attributed, and corrections are made visibly rather than buried in the desert.

Label the lens.

News, analysis, opinion, satire, sponsored work, and AI-assisted imagery are identified clearly.

Punch toward power.

Satire interrogates institutions and hypocrisy. It does not turn vulnerable communities into scenery or targets.
